# Data attribution and licence

## Source

The legislation passages and question/answer pairs in this folder are a subset of the
**Open Australian Legal QA** dataset (Hugging Face: `umarbutler/open-australian-legal-qa`), which is
distributed under the same licence as the **Open Australian Legal Corpus**. The Open Australian
Legal Corpus is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licence
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>). The questions and answers were generated by GPT-4
from the corpus by the dataset's author.

Only records whose source material is itself licensed under CC BY 4.0 are included
(Commonwealth and state legislation). **All case law has been excluded**, because the courts'
own terms restrict reproduction.

## Changes made

This material has been modified. The transformations are listed in `manifest.json`
(`transformations`): licensed-source filtering, Unicode and whitespace normalisation, repair of a
mis-encoded apostrophe, de-duplication, stable identifiers, and a deterministic dev/test split.

**Adversarial overlay.** The files in `adversarial/` are *modified copies* of real passages into
which synthetic attack text (prompt-injection, phishing and markup payloads) has been deliberately
inserted for security testing. They are labelled, kept separate from the clean corpus, and loaded
only in the `demo` and `eval` profiles. The inserted text is ours and is not part of the original
legislation. All addresses, phone numbers and identifiers in it are fictitious (reserved
`.example` domains, a non-working number range).

The hand-written files in `sources/` (out-of-corpus questions, direct-injection questions and the
attack catalogue) are original to this repository.
